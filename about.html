About Our Store

Discover the story behind Coffee Overflow — where great coffee meets good company.

Our Mission

At Coffee Overflow, our mission is simple: make every cup a good one. We offer fresh-roasted coffee blends and the mugs to enjoy them in, so your morning ritual feels a little more special.

From choosing our beans to picking out each mug, we focus on quality you can taste and a shopping experience that is easy from the first click to the last sip.

Our Core Values

Sustainability

We favor responsibly sourced beans so every cup is good for growers and for the planet.

Quality

Fresh blends and well-made mugs, checked carefully before they reach your door.

Craft

Small batches and careful details — because a great cup is made with care.
